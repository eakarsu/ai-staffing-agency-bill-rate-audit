# Staffing Agency Bill-Rate Audit

Audit contingent labor invoices across pay rates, markups, overtime, tenure discounts, conversions, and expenses.

**Primary buyer:** Enterprise HR and procurement teams. **Evidence:** staffing agreements, requisitions, workers, pay rates, markups, timecards, overtime, holidays, conversions, expenses, invoices, and credits.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Staffing agreement library
- Worker assignment registry
- Approved pay rate control
- Markup calculation
- Timecard ingestion
- Overtime rate validation
- Holiday premium audit
- Tenure discount tracking
- Conversion fee calculation
- Expense policy validation
- Duplicate worker billing
- Invoice recalculation
- Agency dispute workflow
- Credit reconciliation
- Agency role analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4726>. API: `5726`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
